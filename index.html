An invitation

An invitation to practitioners of transformation

Dear practitioner,

You can hold a silence for forty seconds in a conference room and make a founder squirm into honesty. You have asked "and what's really going on here?" more times than you can count.

And yet, your own pricing is "let's see." Your bio has four nouns and a slash. Your pipeline is a mix of hope and the one client who keeps referring you. The cobbler's children, as they say, have excellent shoes for everyone else.

We say this with love. We are the cobblers too.

For six years, Co.labx has used the lessons of sport to help purpose-driven organisations grow. Looking back, we were never really a small team. At any moment, 15-20 people stood beside us, and the best work happened when practitioners of different crafts stood on the same field.

So now we are doing it on purpose. By 2030, we want to be a trusted transformation partner to organisations that put purpose, people, planet and profit together, and we can't get there alone.

An unusual bet we're making

We are opening our playbooks and our client work to a circle of practitioners, including people who might otherwise be our competition. Sharing client relationships isn't our default, and we know it isn't yours. We'd rather begin with trust than ask you to earn it.

Whether you are stepping into independent practice or years into it and tired of sharpening your craft alone, this is a circle, not a class. We begin in December, with live work and peer practice through March, and the circle is meant to outlast the dates.

If you have ever made room for others to see what they couldn't see before, we'd love to make some room for you.

Not sure yet? Keep reading, we won't take it personally.

With warmth,

Arhan, Shruti, Jayasudha, Vimal, Saahil and Monica
