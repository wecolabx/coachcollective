An invitation

To all practitioners transforming the future of work

Dear friends,

You know who you are.

One of the rare few who care not just about achieving external results but about shifting the inner conditions that allow new possibilities to emerge.

This letter is for you.

You know those moments well. A team finally says what it has avoided for months. An entrepreneur sees a pattern they didn’t see before. A group makes its way through a hard conversation. You help make such moments possible: by asking the right questions, holding the silence when it gets uncomfortable, and presencing a possibility people felt but hadn’t yet articulated.

You may call yourself a coach, facilitator, mentor, team builder, or org leader. Or maybe you use none of those labels. What matters is the practice: helping people and organisations transform how they see, relate and create together.

And yet, what’s also true is that this work can be lonely. The path is unclear. Love for the craft sits beside very real questions. How do I do more of this? How do I reach more people? How do I get paid for it? How do I explain what I do? Some days those questions energise. Some days they weigh heavy.

We say this with great care. We are those people too.

For six years, Co.labx has helped purpose-driven entrepreneurs grow their people, impact and business. During this time, we have worked with and learned from 100+ teams across sectors and industries.

We always saw ourselves as a tiny team. Looking back, we weren’t. At any given moment, 15–20 people stood beside us: entrepreneurs, facilitators, coaches, designers, techies. Our best work happened when practitioners of different crafts stood on the same field.

We now want to honour that way of working.

Why so?

Because an outdated leadership playbook is holding organisations back from addressing the world’s most complex problems. And what the world needs is not bigger or better orgs but a new species of organisation – conscious, courageous, decentralised, and built for the 21st century.

Our aspiration at Co.labx is to be trusted transformation partners to visionary leaders creating such orgs, who are committed to balancing purpose, people, profit and planet. But we can’t get there alone.

So we are now choosing to take a bold step: investing in our competition.

We are opening up our work, our clients, our playbooks and our networks to practitioners who want to build and grow together, even if on the surface it might seem like we are playing for the same piece of the pie.

Whether you are taking the first steps toward an independent practice, or are years in and tired of building alone, this letter is for you. We invite ten of you to join the six of us on a quest to reimagine the future of work and organisations.

If this invitation speaks to you, we welcome you to take the next step.

Yes, I’m in

Not sure yet? Keep reading. We won’t take it personally. :)

With love,
Arhan, Shruti, Jayasudha, Vimal, Saahil and Monica

The details

Welcome to the circle

Well, almost. :) Here is what you’d be signing up for, what we commit to, what we ask of you, and how we get to know each other.

What this is

A circle of 16 practitioners (6 of us + 10 of you) committed to the art and practice of individual, team and org transformation.

We give, grow and receive, and partner on concrete projects. Each of you is supported by mentors whose strengths complement yours across the areas of coaching, facilitation, business, and growth.

  • December to March: live client projects, paid co-facilitation, a breakthrough project of your choosing, and peer practice.
  • Beyond March: a long-term partnership, and a shared journey to 2030 and a world of transformative work & regenerative organisations.

What you’ll build

  • Your craft: playbooks for closing clients, deepening relationships with CEOs and leadership, coaching individuals and teams, and designing new offerings.
  • Your business: clarity on how to position yourself, create the right offerings, price your work and build a pipeline.
  • A breakthrough possibility: this could be a new client, a new offering or a new partnership, towards growing sustainably.
  • Your support system: peers and mentors who challenge, support and champion you.

What this is not: a course, a classroom, a stop-gap exploration, or a shortcut to more clients.

What we’re investing

You

3–4 hours a week on average (which might increase if we work on shared client projects together), plus your presence at an in-person gathering in Bengaluru at the home of one of our trusted partners, the Dravid Centre for Sports Excellence (3 days, 2 nights). It costs about ₹[15,000 – 20,000] per person on actuals for sports facilities, lodging and food. Travel is yours.

Us

Our experience, playbooks, time and relationships. The community is a shared investment and we make no money on it.

Paid work

The opportunity to share revenue on live client projects, with travel and logistics covered. This isn’t guaranteed.
