Who we are

An unusual bet

We know this work needs more people who can hold complexity and work with the human side of change. So we're investing in making our competition better.

200+ teams · 6 years

Co.labx is a transformation practice. Over the last six years, we've worked with 200+ teams, using the lessons of sport to help purpose-driven organisations grow their people, their impact and their business. We'd like to open our playbooks and client work to a small circle of practitioners.

Is this for you?

This is an invitation for you if…

You've already decided this matters. We see you.

You're about to go full-time on your own, or just have.

You have 3+ years of experience as a coach, facilitator, business coach, sports coach or systems practitioner, inside organisations or outside them.

You take the work seriously and yourself a little less so.

You don't need everything figured out. Bring your experience, your questions, and the thing you've been wanting to try. We'll bring ours.

The Collective

Ten practitioners. Four months.

A regenerative community where everyone gives and everyone grows.

We're not selecting you. We're inviting you, and you decide whether you're ready to invest your time, presence and resources. Our criteria are open, so you can judge your own fit before you apply.

Think of a community, not a class. A boardroom facilitator, a business coach and a sports coach each read the same moment differently, and that mix is the point.

What you'll build

Run your business

Your breakthrough, yours

A first client, a new offering, a practice that finally feels sustainable. You choose it. Your practice and your clients stay yours.

A community to get you there

Nine peers who'll challenge, refer and back you, in the huddle and on the field, well beyond the four months.

What we're each investing

You're investing

Your time. 3–4 hours a week for the community, plus 3–4 more if you co-facilitate on a client engagement.

Your craft. Your experience, your questions, and the thing you've been wanting to try.

Your money. The first in-person workshop is paid on actuals for facilities, lodging and food, about ₹10,000 per person. Travel is yours.

We're investing

Our experience, knowledge and craft. Our playbooks and client work, opened to the circle.

Our time and money. The programme itself is free, and we take no margin on the workshop.

Paid work

Around ₹10,000–20,000 per day for co-facilitation, with travel and logistics covered. It isn't guaranteed.

The win-win-win

When one of us grows, we all do.

You build a practice that sustains you. The organisations you serve get stronger. The ecosystem grows, and that includes us. We'll be honest about what we hope to gain: better ways of serving our clients, trusted collaborators, and a stronger field.
